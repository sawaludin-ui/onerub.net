## Current evidence limitations

A read-only chain scan during hardening observed 30 event-emitting successful transactions, 12 interacting wallet addresses, and 11 current Agent ID holders at snapshot block134963053 across the original Office and marketplace V1 contracts. This includes a nonholder deployer and is not a count of paid marketplace tasks, historical holder eligibility, or organizer-approved participation. Office activity is distinct from marketplace work. It does not establish the30-address requirement.

The announcement describes30addresses in Innovation eligibility, while the official form introduction states a general project eligibility condition of a live product with at least30Agent ID addresses interacting during the Submission Period. Treat the stricter form introduction as a gate until clarified; do not promise a below-threshold submission is eligible.

No submission receipt or award is claimed. Local tests and test fixtures are not mainnet traction. No accounts, volume, jobs or payments should be fabricated to fill the gap.
